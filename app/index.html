Reading the badges

  • NEW LOW Lowest price seen for this item this year.
  • STRONG A deep cut or a big dollar saving on a good product.
  • FAIR A genuine discount, but modest — worth it if you already wanted it.
  • ROUTINE A small cut, or a price this item hits often. Don’t rush.

How we check a deal

  1. Read the price. Every sale price here was read on 27 November 2026, either on the store’s own product page or in a deals roundup that names the store and the price. The card says which.
  2. Check the “usual” price. Best Buy shows a “comparable value” — the store’s own reference number, not proof the item ever sold for that much. Other stores show a list price. We label which one it is, and judge the deal by the sale price itself.
  3. Call it honestly. A 7% cut on a brand-new laptop is a routine sale, not a bargain. A 67% cut on flagship headphones is a strong price. The evidence is on the card, so you can disagree with us.
  4. Link to the product. Every “View at” button goes to that product at that store — not a search page, not a homepage.

Prices move fast on Black Friday and stock runs out. Check the live listing before you pay — if the price has changed, the deal has changed. This site is not affiliated with any store named here.
