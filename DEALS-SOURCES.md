# Deal sources — audit notes (not part of the site)

All prices were checked on Black Friday, 27 November 2026, around 06:30 UTC
(14:30 CST). "Store page" means the store's own product page as fetched that
day; "roundup" means a deals roundup that names the store and the price.
Amazon's product pages returned HTTP 403 to this machine's page fetcher, so
for Amazon deals the price comes from The Verge's roundup (which links to the
Amazon listing) and the link is the roundup link's resolved Amazon destination.

Best Buy shows a "comparable value" next to sale prices instead of a simple
"was" price. It is the store's reference number, not proof the item ever sold
for that much — the site labels it "Comp. value" and judges the deal by the
sale price itself.
